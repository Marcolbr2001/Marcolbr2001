<h1 align="center">Hi 👋, I'm Marco La Barbera</h1>
<h3 align="center">AI Chip Researcher developing RISC-V processors for Satellite Payloads 🛰️🤖</h3>

- 🎓 PhD student at the **University of Bologna**, part of the [PULP Platform](https://pulp-platform.org/) group, working on a **European Space Agency (ESA)** project

- 🛰️ Designing RISC-V-based processors to run AI workloads on satellite payloads, and studying how deep learning models hold up against radiation-induced bit-flips (single-event upsets) through fault-injection campaigns

- 🔭 **Background:** M.Sc. in Electronics Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, with an exchange period at Chalmers University of Technology 🇸🇪. My Master's thesis was an ESA collaboration on a space dust-sensor system: I designed the PCB (EMC analysis, component selection, Cadence simulations, microcontroller choice), developed firmware for an STM32 Arm-based microcontroller, and ran a test campaign with lunar simulant particles at ESA/ESTEC

- 🚀 Main interests: **FPGA/ASIC design, computer architecture, RISC-V, VHDL, Verilog, HLS**, and energy-efficient AI hardware for space

- 💡 I love building systems from the ground up, from architecture to implementation
